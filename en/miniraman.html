miniRaman Spectrometer

The world’s most compact research-grade Raman spectrometer

An innovative portable Raman spectrometer with a patented built-in reference channel. Real-time automatic calibration compensates for laser wavelength drift, delivering maximum accuracy, reproducibility and speed of analysis both in the laboratory and in the field.

Key advantages

Patented built-in calibration

Continuous automatic correction of Raman shift and intensity — no manual calibration before work.

Ultra-compact size

Just 360 g at 105 × 50 × 45 mm.

Research grade

High sensitivity and spectral detail in a portable format.

Battery life and mobility

Up to 4 hours on battery, Bluetooth and USB support.

Wide choice of models

Standard measurements, high-sensitivity SERS analysis and dual-wavelength 660 / 785 nm configurations.
